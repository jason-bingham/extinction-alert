Extinction Alert

Privacy Policy

Data collection

Extinction Alert does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing information. There are no analytics, no remote servers, and no third-party services involved in the operation of this extension.

Local storage

All settings — alert style, reminder interval, pause state, and today's alert count — are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions

The extension requests the tabs permission and <all_urls> host access solely to deliver overlay alerts to the active browser tab. No tab URLs, page content, or user activity is recorded or transmitted.
