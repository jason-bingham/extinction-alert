# Extinction Alert – Privacy Policy

**Extinction Alert does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing information.**

All settings (alert style, interval, pause state, and today's alert count) are stored locally on your device using `chrome.storage` and never leave it.

The extension uses the `tabs` permission and `<all_urls>` host permission solely to deliver overlay alerts to the active browser tab. No tab URLs, page content, or user activity is recorded or sent anywhere.

There are no analytics, no remote servers, and no third-party services involved in the operation of this extension.
